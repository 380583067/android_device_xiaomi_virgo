## Repairs

1. `include/linux/compiler-gcc.h` previously routed GCC 5+ into `compiler-gcc4.h`.
   With GCC 7.5, the GCC 4 minor-version tests silently disabled `__visible`.
   Select the existing GCC 5+ attribute definitions and retain object-size checking.
2. The first GCC 7.5 link failed: `ERROR: modpost: Found 13 section mismatch(es).`
   Keep 12 device-match tables in permanent read-only storage, and retain the GDSC counter.
   Drivers continue to hold references to these objects after initialization.
   The final modpost check passes with zero section mismatches; error checking remains enabled.
3. At the user's request, the BoardConfig migration was withdrawn. ROM builds retain the original bundled GCC 4.9 compiler and `-j8`. Standalone GCC 7.5 builds still use 18 jobs through the separate script.
4. Added `device/xiaomi/virgo/build-kernel-gcc75.sh` to reproduce the standalone build from the known-booting configuration.
